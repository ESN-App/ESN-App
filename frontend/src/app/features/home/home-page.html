Meet ESN Gdańsk

ESN Gdańsk is a volunteer-led student association that helps international students feel at home from their first days in the city.

We connect people across cultures through events, trips, local knowledge and everyday support. Our goal is simple: make your exchange easier, more social and full of experiences worth remembering.

  • An international community
